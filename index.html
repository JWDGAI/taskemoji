taskemoji

taskemoji adds emoji to your Google Tasks, so priority, type and rhythm show at a glance on every device.

You typep1 -tb draft the launch plan
It becomes🔴 🗄️ draft the launch plan

What it does

  • Turns short codes typed in a task title into emoji: p1 to p4 for priority, -tb and similar for the kind of task, -g and similar for a color label.
  • Reads markers in a list's name, such as +🎯, and applies them to every task in that list.
  • Sorts each list by priority and sets due dates from list names such as "Today" or "This week".
  • Adds repeating emoji over time, for example one more 🎉 each week a task has been around.

Everything is written back into your own Google Tasks, so the result appears in the Tasks app, in Google Calendar and on your phone.

Who it is for

taskemoji is a small personal tool. It runs inside its owner's Google account as a Google Apps Script project and is not a public service.

Permissions and your data

To do its job, taskemoji asks for permission to view and manage Google Tasks and to run on a schedule. It does not send your tasks anywhere else. The details are in the privacy policy.
